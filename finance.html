Cash Flow & Scenario Modelling

Problem

The business was launching a new product with significantly different payment terms to its existing revenue streams. This created a need to forecast cash much further ahead, with greater visibility of how the new product could affect the overall cash position.

Solution

Built a detailed Excel cash flow model to forecast the business's income, outgoings and cash position over a longer time horizon, alongside a supporting management dashboard to provide a clear view of forecast liquidity and cash risk.

Added scenario modelling to assess how different New Product revenue and cost assumptions could affect the overall business cash position.

Key Insights / Impact

  • Identified a specific month where the business was particularly exposed to cash flow risk.
  • Provided visibility of the timing and scale of the potential cash shortfall, allowing management to plan accordingly.
