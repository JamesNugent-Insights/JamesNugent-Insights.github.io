CAPEX Approval Process Improvement

Problem

  • Operations teams found the existing CAPEX approval process slow and frustrating.
  • Senior Finance often did not receive all the information needed to make informed decisions first time, resulting in repeated questions and delays.
  • The process also varied across the UK, Germany and Poland.

Solution

  • Worked with the Head Engineer in Poland to redesign the CAPEX approval process around the information Senior Finance needed to make decisions.
  • Developed a standardised PowerPoint pack that answered key questions upfront and used images to clearly show both existing equipment and proposed replacements.
  • Following positive feedback from Senior Finance, the new process was adopted across Germany and the UK.

Impact

  • Significantly reduced CAPEX approval times by providing decision-makers with the information they needed upfront.
  • Improved consistency across the UK, Germany and Poland through a standardised approval process.
  • Improved working relationships between Operations and Finance by creating a clearer and more efficient process.
